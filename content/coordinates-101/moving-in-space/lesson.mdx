# What are X, Y, and Z?

Every point in 3D space can be described with three numbers: **X**, **Y**, and **Z**.
Think of them as three independent directions you can move in:

- **X** (red) — left and right
- **Y** (green) — up and down
- **Z** (blue) — forward and backward

Together, `(x, y, z)` pins down exactly one location in space, no matter how big that space is.
The point where all three axes cross — `(0, 0, 0)` — is called the **origin**.
